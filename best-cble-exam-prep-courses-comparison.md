## Why people look past the traditional course

Based on the provider's published terms (ST&R, checked 2026-11-13):

- **Price:** $1,500 for the course, with the HTSUS and 19 CFR books bought separately.
- **Time:** ST&R says to plan for 100 hours of course-related work, about 40 of instruction and 60+ of homework.
- **No refund after the course starts.** Cancellations need to arrive at least 5 business days before course materials open. Deferrals are case by case.
- **Only two start dates a year.** The course starts two months before each exam, so if you miss registration you wait for the next cycle.
- **Fixed schedule.** Live sessions are scheduled, though recordings stay available afterward.

None of these is a flaw if the course fits your life. They are the reasons a self-funded candidate or a working professional might want a cheaper, self-paced option.

## Where the traditional course is the better choice

Pick the instructor-led course if any of these describe you:

1. **You want a real person to ask.** ST&R's course includes live sessions for review and questions, taught by an attorney with 20 years of teaching this course and a seat on the working group that helps CBP improve the exam. An AI tutor is not a substitute for a live instructor.
2. **You need structure and accountability.** A fixed schedule with homework and practice exams keeps people on track. Self-paced tools put that on you.
3. **You want exam-mechanics coaching.** ST&R covers highlighting and tabbing strategy, time management, and navigating reference materials during the open-book test. Several of its students credit this coaching for their pass.
4. **You want a second attempt covered.** ST&R offers a half-price retake if you complete the course and do not pass.
5. **Your employer pays and prefers an established program.**

## FAQ

**Can I pass the CBLE without a course?**
Yes. There are no education or experience prerequisites, and CBP publishes past exams with answer keys for free. Pass rates are low in every case (22% for April 2026), so the amount of practice matters more than the format.

**Is the CBLE open book?**
Yes. You may bring printed materials but no electronic devices (ST&R, checked 2026-11-13).

**Can I use an AI tutor during the exam?**
No. Use it to study; you cannot bring devices into the exam.

**How much does the whole process cost?**
Exam fee of $390, plus books, plus whatever prep you choose. See the cost table above.
